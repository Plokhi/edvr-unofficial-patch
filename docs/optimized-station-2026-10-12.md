## What ships

## Trigger (v14.23): the game's pad state

The seal trigger is the game's own pad state, read by the pad-state probe (`pad_state_probe.cpp`, `pad_state_core.h`). The own pad is the object the docking reader r0 was last called on in this dock (`GameTrack`); it is reset at undock and at a hangar change, and a switch to another object is logged once. "In the hangar" is the own pad's r1 equal to 1, read fresh (within 1 s). A seal needs: in the hangar, the setting on, Status docked, a valid pad pick and cell, and the camera in the bay for 10 frames. It is immediate: no stillness clock, no load-in or arrival rule, no descent observation, no fallback.

Releases: setting off, undocked, the camera out for 10 frames, the mechanism moved (the latch), the pad moved (ShellMotion), the hangar changed, and the own pad leaving the hangar (r1 1 to 0). After a mechanism, pad-moved or left-hangar release, the next seal needs an r1 0 then 1 edge. A camera or setting release needs none.

Sun: off while the own pad's r0 is 1 (moving in) or 3 (the descent), read fresh. Back on when the pad is out of the hangar, r0 is neither 1 nor 3, and the pad is raised (the geometric height test), or on undock, setting off, or a hangar change. The outpost surface (r0 0) never turns it off.

Lines: the waiting line is `optimized station: waiting (docked=yes, own pad @0abc40, r0=1 r1=1, in hangar=yes, camera=yes, cell=built)`, or `... game pad state unknown (no r0 on this dock) ...`. The sun line is `... sun light inside the hangar off (<why>; r0=1 r1=1; Status docked=yes)`. The probe's own-pad line at a seal or release is `pad state at seal: own pad obj @... r0=.. r1=.. r2=.. r3=.. byte=.. occ=.. o2c=.. o2f=..`, or `own pad unknown`.

Kept for the trigger: the latch (the mechanism release), `ShellMotion` (the pad-moved release), the pick (`pickOwnPad`, `pickOwnPadSticky`), the shell pick (`pickShell`), the held-cell rules (`keepNamedCell`, `heldCellDrops`, `ownSphereKeeps`, `inBayVolume`, `boxCoordsOf`), and the pad-frame release (`padMovedAway`, `localizeCell`, `worldizeCell`). Removed in v14.23: the geometric seal state (`SealState` load-in, moved and still fields), the bay-still and 30 s fallback, `DockWatch` (arrival and descent), `PadStillWatch` (turn), the pad-track instrument, and `ShellHeightSources`.

Core (pure logic, `src/d3d11/optimized_station_core.h`): the trigger (`sealStep`, `sunStep`, `GameTrack`, `gameWaitingLine`), the own-pad pick with the near-tie rule and the sticky state (`pickOwnPad`, `pickOwnPadSticky`), the shell pick (`pickShell`), the held-cell rules (`keepNamedCell`, `heldCellDrops`, `ownSphereKeeps`, `inBayVolume`, `boxCoordsOf`), and the pad-frame release (`padMovedAway`, `localizeCell`, `worldizeCell`).

Engine (`src/d3d11/optimized_station.cpp`): the store judgement, the rig and producer vetoes, the held-cell refresh each frame, the seal tick, and the two status kinds (one line per state change, a 30 s summary line).

Hooks: `store_hook.cpp` (path A, store observer), `kinematic_eval_hook.cpp` (producer-B veto on the engine-motion bracket of 0x4312E00; rig early veto on `rigEvalObserved` emulating Elite's off-screen branch), `vscreen.cpp` (sun-pass skip while sealed), `journal_watch.cpp` (Flags bit 0 as docked), `view_source.cpp` (explorer branch removed).
